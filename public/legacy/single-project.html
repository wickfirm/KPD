Seven X Seven

An address shaped
by restraint

Seven X Seven Residences sits within Meydan Horizon, planned around measured city access and the quiet rhythm of everyday living. The brief begins with how the home is used - arrival, light, and privacy - and lets the architecture follow.

Meydan Horizon

Meydan Horizon places Seven X Seven within a measured urban district shaped by movement, water, and direct access to Dubai's business and cultural centers. The address gives residents a quieter daily rhythm while keeping Downtown, DIFC, DXB, and Ras Al Khor within practical reach.
